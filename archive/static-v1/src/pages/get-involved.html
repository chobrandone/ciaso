Volunteer

Give your time. Make someone feel at home.

Volunteers play a vital role in helping newcomers feel at home in Canada. By giving your time and skills, you support families and individuals as they adjust to a new culture, find resources and build brighter futures.

Together, we create a welcoming community where every newcomer feels supported and empowered. Ways to help include:

  • Mentoring – share your professional and personal experience
  • Language support – help newcomers practise and build confidence
  • Event coordination – help run workshops and community events
  • Administrative assistance – keep our programs running smoothly
