What do you need help with?

Settling in Canada

CIASO can help with:

  • Food, clothing and shelter support for families in need
  • Guidance on adapting to life in Canada
  • Connections to community resources and services
  • Information sessions on topics such as taxes and benefits
Send us a messageLearn more about Settle

Finding employment

CIASO can help with:

  • Job training, skills development and career guidance
  • Resume and cover-letter review
  • Interview preparation and practice
  • Career workshops and job-search support
Send us a messageLearn more about Work

Skills, education or business

CIASO can help with:

  • Educational resources, supplies and tutoring for children
  • Entrepreneurship training and small business support
  • Professional development workshops
  • English as a Second Language (ask us about current availability)
Send us a messageLearn more about Grow

Connecting with community

CIASO can help with:

  • Networking events and workshops
  • Mentorship, coaching and one-on-one advice
  • Youth, minority and elderly support programs
Send us a messageLearn more about Belong

Family & wellbeing support

CIASO can help with:

  • Individual counselling and group sessions
  • Wellness and mental health support
  • Social and personal development programs

In an emergency, call 911. If you are in crisis or thinking about suicide, call or text 988 (Suicide Crisis Helpline, available 24/7 across Canada). CIASO cannot respond to emergencies.
