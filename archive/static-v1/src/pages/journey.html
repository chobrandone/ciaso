Your CIASO Journey

Six stages. One direction: forward.

Building a life in a new country happens one step at a time. The CIASO Journey is a simple way to see where you are, what could come next, and how we can help along the way.

Discover→Connect→Equip→Work→Grow→Thrive

Journey overview

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understand your new environment and the opportunities available to you.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Find community, trusted resources, mentors, and meaningful relationships.

  3. 03

    Equip

    Develop skills, knowledge, confidence, and practical tools.

  4. 04

    Work

    Pursue career opportunities and greater economic independence.

  5. 05

    Grow

    Keep developing personally, professionally, and socially.

  6. 06

    Thrive

    Become an active, confident contributor to your community.

Every journey is different. These stages are a guide, not a checklist. You might start at “Work”, return to “Connect” after a move, or be at several stages at once. Wherever you are, CIASO can help you find your next step.

Stage by stage

How CIASO supports each stage.

01

Discover

What this looks like

Getting oriented: understanding how things work in Canada, what services exist, and what opportunities are open to you and your family.

How CIASO can help

  • Settlement guidance and connection to community resources
  • Newcomer information sessions, such as taxes and benefits
  • Basic necessities support for families in need

02

Connect

What this looks like

Building your network: meeting people, finding trusted sources of help, and beginning to feel part of a community.

How CIASO can help

  • Networking at workshops and events
  • Mentorship, coaching and one-on-one advice
  • Youth, minority and elderly support programs

03

Equip

What this looks like

Getting ready: strengthening skills and knowledge, and preparing the tools you need – from a Canadian-style resume to confidence in interviews.

How CIASO can help

  • Resume, cover-letter and interview preparation
  • Skills development and professional development workshops
  • ESL (ask about current availability)

04

Work

What this looks like

Pursuing opportunity: searching and applying for jobs, starting a business, and building economic independence.

How CIASO can help

  • Job training, employment placement and career guidance
  • Sharing reputable, relevant job opportunities
  • Entrepreneurship training

05

Grow

What this looks like

Moving forward: advancing your career, continuing to learn, and supporting your children’s education.

How CIASO can help

  • Career workshops on LinkedIn, credentials and career pathways
  • Educational resources and tutoring for children
  • Social and personal development programs

06

Thrive

What this looks like

Giving back: living with confidence and wellbeing, and becoming a resource for others in your community.

How CIASO can help

  • Counselling and wellness programs
  • Volunteer and mentoring opportunities
  • Share your story to inspire others
