Our programs

Support for every step of your journey.

CIASO’s programs and services are organized into five pillars – Settle, Work, Grow, Belong and Thrive. You don’t need to know which one fits. Tell us what you need and we’ll help you find the right support.

01 · Settle

Find your footing. Feel at home.

Guidance, resources and practical support to help you and your family navigate life in Canada.

Basic necessities support

Food, clothing and shelter support for newcomers and families in need.

Settlement guidance

Personalized assistance and guidance on adapting to life in Canada, so you feel supported throughout your settlement and integration journey.

Connection to community resources

We take time to understand your situation and connect you to the right support – at CIASO or in the wider community.

Newcomer information sessions

Sessions on practical topics such as understanding the Canadian tax system and accessing government benefits.

Get Settlement Support

02 · Work

Turn your potential into opportunity.

Build the tools, confidence and connections you need to navigate the Canadian job market and pursue your career goals.

Job training & employment support

Skills development, employment placement and career guidance to foster economic inclusion.

Resume & cover-letter review

Learn how to write strong, tailored applications – with resume and cover-letter review and practical workshops.

Interview preparation

Interview practice, tips and one-on-one preparation to help you present yourself with confidence.

Career workshops

Regular sessions on the Canadian job market, LinkedIn, building Canadian experience and career pathways in fields such as healthcare.

Job-search support

Guidance through the job-search process, including sharing reputable and relevant job opportunities.

Get Employment SupportSee past career workshops

03 · Grow

Keep learning. Keep moving forward.

Develop your skills, strengthen your knowledge and discover new pathways for personal and professional growth.

Educational resources for children

School supplies, tutoring and learning support for children.

Entrepreneurship training

Support for small business creation and economic inclusion, including sessions on business planning, financing and selling online.

Professional development

Workshops on transferable skills, credentials and bridging programs for internationally trained professionals.

English as a Second Language (ESL)

ESL instruction is one of the services you can ask about.

Build connections that make a difference.

Connect with people, mentors, volunteers and community resources that help you build meaningful relationships and feel part of your community.

Networking opportunities

Workshops and events where you can meet professionals, employers and other newcomers.

Mentorship & coaching

Encouragement, coaching and one-on-one advice from people who understand the newcomer journey.

Youth, minority & elderly support programs

Initiatives promoting empowerment, inclusion and personal development.

Volunteer opportunities

Give back by mentoring, providing language support, coordinating events or helping with administration.

05 · Thrive

Build confidence. Create your future.

Move beyond settling in. Build the confidence, wellbeing and opportunities that support long-term participation and success.

Counselling & wellness programs

Individual counselling, group sessions and mental health support to help individuals and families overcome challenges and build resilience.

Social & personal development

Programs that build confidence, life skills and community connections.

Economic empowerment

Entrepreneurial training and resources that support economic development and self-sufficiency.
