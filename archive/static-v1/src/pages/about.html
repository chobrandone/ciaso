About CIASO

A bridge between newcomers and opportunity.

CIASO is more than a settlement organization. We are a bridge between newcomers and opportunity – connecting individuals and families to the resources, skills, relationships and support they need to build meaningful, confident and successful lives in Canada.

Who we are

Empowering families. Building futures.

The Canada Integration and Settlement Organization (CIASO) is a nonprofit organization based in Alberta and incorporated in Canada. We are dedicated to enhancing the quality of life for newcomers and Canadians alike, serving families and individuals in Alberta in particular and across Canada.

We exist to ensure that every individual and family feels supported, empowered and integrated – able to thrive in their new community. Our goal is to make services accessible, inclusive and empowering for everyone.

Our vision

A welcoming, inclusive society where every newcomer and their family thrives with dignity, confidence, and connection – where their skills are recognized, their contributions are valued, and their experiences are utilized to empower communities.

Our values

What guides our work.

Dignity & respect

We believe every individual deserves to be treated with compassion, respect, and dignity.

Diversity & inclusion

We are committed to removing systemic barriers and creating welcoming, inclusive spaces for all newcomers.

Empowerment

We support individuals in building confidence and self-sufficiency so they can thrive in their new communities.

Our approach

We don’t just help newcomers settle in Canada. We help them build a future.

The challenge

Barriers to full participation

Newcomers and their families may encounter barriers to employment, education, integration, community connection and economic participation – from the demand for “Canadian experience” to unfamiliar systems and the loss of familiar networks.

Our response

Connection to the right support

CIASO connects people to practical support, resources, skills development, relationships and pathways – through personalized guidance, workshops, mentorship and partnerships with community organizations.

Intended outcomes

Confident, connected lives

We work so that individuals and families can strengthen their skills, build networks, pursue employment and education, develop confidence, and participate more fully in community and economic life.
