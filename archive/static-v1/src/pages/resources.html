FAQ

Common questions, answered.

What is CIASO?

CIASO (Canada Integration and Settlement Organization) is a nonprofit based in Alberta dedicated to supporting newcomers and Canadians. We help individuals and families feel safe, supported, and empowered to thrive in their community.

Who can access CIASO’s services?

Our programs are available to newcomers, families, youth, minorities, the elderly, and anyone seeking support with settlement, integration, personal growth, or wellness in Alberta. Some workshops are offered online. Eligibility can vary by program – ask us and we’ll help you find the right fit.

How do I get started?

Use our Get Support page, email admin@ciaso.ca, call us, or visit our office. We’ll schedule a consultation to understand your needs and connect you to the right support.

What is the consultation process like?

We start by understanding your goals and personal situation. Then we guide you, step by step, through the options and support that fit best.

How can I get help with employment or job training?

CIASO offers programs for skills development, employment training and placement, plus resume, cover-letter and interview support. Contact us to find out which program best fits your needs and how to enroll.

Can CIASO help with mental health or family challenges?

Yes. We provide counselling, group sessions and wellness programs to help individuals and families overcome challenges and build resilience. In an emergency, call 911. If you are in crisis, call or text 988, available 24/7.

How can I volunteer or partner with CIASO?

Visit Get Involved to apply as a volunteer or start a partnership conversation.

Didn’t find your answer?

Our team is happy to help. Contact CIASO or call 587-813-5155.
