Who we are

More than settlement. A pathway to possibility.

Starting over in a new country comes with questions, challenges, and new opportunities. CIASO is an Alberta-based nonprofit that helps newcomers and their families navigate their journey by connecting them with practical support, career development, learning opportunities, and meaningful community relationships.

Whether you are finding your footing, preparing for employment, developing new skills, or building your network, CIASO is here to help you take your next step.

Who we serve

Support for every stage of your new life.

CIASO serves newcomers, families and community members, primarily in Alberta, with some workshops offered online. Programs vary – tell us what you need and we’ll help you find the right fit.

Newcomers & families

Guidance on adapting to life in Canada, community resources, and essential support for families in need.

Job seekers

Resume and cover-letter help, interview preparation, job training and career guidance.

Internationally trained professionals

Workshops on transferable skills, Canadian experience and pathways in fields such as healthcare.

Students & children

Career workshops for international students, and educational resources, supplies and tutoring for children.

Aspiring entrepreneurs

Entrepreneurship training and resources to support small business creation.

Youth, seniors & minority communities

Programs promoting empowerment, inclusion and personal development.

Volunteers & community members

Mentoring, language support, events and administration – your time makes a real difference.
